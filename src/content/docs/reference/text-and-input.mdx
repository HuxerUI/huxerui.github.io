Declared by `<huxerui/text.h>`, `<huxerui/text_input.h>`, `<huxerui/validation.h>`, and `<huxerui/clipboard.h>`.

## Fonts and styles

- `FontFamilyKind`: `System`, `Monospace`, or `Named`.
- `FontWeight`: `Thin`, `ExtraLight`, `Light`, `Regular`, `Medium`, `SemiBold`, `Bold`, `ExtraBold`, or `Black`.
- `FontSlant`: `Normal` or `Italic`.
- `Font`: `System(size)`, named/fallback family construction, and fluent size/weight/slant updates; equality compares descriptor values.
- `TextDecoration`: `None`, `Underline`, `StrikeThrough` flags; bitwise `|`, `&`, and `HasTextDecoration` support combinations.
- `TextStyle`: `font`, `foreground`, and `decoration`.

## Shaping and layout

- `TextDirection`: `Auto`, `LeftToRight`, or `RightToLeft`.
- `TextShapingOptions`: locale/language and direction inputs.
- `TextAlign`: `Leading`, `Center`, or `Trailing`.
- `TextWrap`: `NoWrap` or `Word`.
- `TextLayoutOptions`: shaping options, alignment, and wrapping; maximum width is the separate `MeasureText` argument.
- `FontMetrics`, `TextRunMetrics`, and `TextLayoutMetrics`: ascent/descent/leading, run geometry, overall size, baselines, and line data.
- `TextMeasurer` platform interface and `UseTextMeasurer()` extension hook.

## Editing values

- `TextOffset` is signed 64-bit; `TextInputSessionId` is unsigned 64-bit.
- `TextAffinity`: `Upstream` or `Downstream`; `TextPosition`, `TextRange`, and `TextSelection` model caret/range state.
- `TextEditingValue` contains UTF-8 text, selection, and composition range with validation/equality helpers.

## IME command boundary

`TextInputCommandKind` values are `SetSelection`, `BeginComposition`, `UpdateComposition`, `CommitText`, `FinishComposition`, `CancelComposition`, and `DeleteSurrounding`.
`TextInputCoordinateSpace` is `Text` or `Composition`; `TextInputUnit` is `Utf16CodeUnit` or `UnicodeCodePoint`.
`TextInputCommand` and `TextInputCommandBatch` carry revisioned platform edits.

- `TextInputType`: `Text`, `Email`, `Number`, `Decimal`, `Phone`, or `Url`.
- `TextCapitalization`: `None`, `Characters`, `Words`, or `Sentences`.
- `TextInputAction`: `Default`, `Done`, `Go`, `Next`, `Search`, `Send`, or `Newline`.
- `TextInputConfiguration`: type, capitalization, action, multiline, secure, autocorrect, and read-only flags.

- `TextInputResultCode`: `Ok`, `SessionMismatch`, `Rejected`, or `ReadOnly`.
- `TextInputSyncAction`: `None`, `Update`, or `Restart`.
- `TextInputEndReason`: `FocusLost`, `ClientRemoved`, `Disabled`, `ReadOnly`, or `RuntimeDestroyed`.
- `TextInputKeyResult`: `Unhandled` or `Handled`.
- State/result structures: `TextInputState`, `TextInputApplyResult`, `TextInputContext`, `TextInputGeometry`, and `TextInputPositionResult`.

`TextInputClient` and `TextSelectionClient` are mounted extension interfaces. `PlatformTextInput` is the adapter interface for begin/update/end session, keyboard, geometry queries, selection menus, and platform synchronization.

## Validation

`ValidationStatus` is `None`, `Valid`, `Invalid`, or `Pending`. `ValidationResult` has named factories and `IsInvalid`. `Required` and `EmailAddress` accept optional localized messages. `Validate(value, rules...)` evaluates until a rule is non-valid.

## Clipboard

`TextEditingAction` is `Cut`, `Copy`, `Paste`, or `SelectAll`. `TextSelectionMenuLabels` supplies localized labels. `PlatformClipboard` defines `ReadText` and `WriteText` for adapters.

## Complete controlled TextField example

```cpp compile="text-field-validation"
#include <huxerui/huxerui.h>

using namespace huxerui;

[[huxerui::composable]]
View AccountEmail() {
  auto email = UseState(TextEditingValue::FromText(""));

  return TextField(email)
      .Label("Email")
      .Placeholder("name@example.com")
      .Variant(TextFieldVariant::Outlined)
      .InputConfiguration({
          .type = TextInputType::Email,
          .action = TextInputAction::Done,
      })
      .Validation(Validate(email->text, Required(), EmailAddress()))
      .OnChanged([email](const TextEditingValue& next) {
        email = next;
      });
}
```

Writing the complete event value preserves selection, affinity, and composition. Validation reports domain state and does not rewrite the edit.
